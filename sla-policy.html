SLA Policy

Every ticket receives a priority at intake. The priority sets the response target and never changes the assigned queue.

Response Targets

Priority Impact Score Respond Within
High 8 to 10 4 hours
Medium 4 to 7 8 hours
Low 1 to 3 24 hours

Escalation

A ticket moves up one priority level when the reporter marks it as a service outage and at least 50 users are affected. Both conditions are required. A High priority ticket cannot move higher.
